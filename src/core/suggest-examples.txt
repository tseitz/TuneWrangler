# Rules
- Name is "Artist - Title" or "Artist - Album - Title". The album slot is optional.
- A remix, flip, edit, bootleg or "freebie" rework is "Remixer - Original Artist - Title". The remixer always comes first.
- A label, uploader account or collective that released someone else's track goes in the album slot: "Artist - Label - Title".
- Several artists are joined with " x " (never "&", ",", "feat.", "ft.", "and"). A featured artist joins the artist slot it belongs to; on a remix, featured artists belong to the original artist slot.
- Remix credits and their brackets come out of the title: "(X Remix)", "(X Flip)", "(X Edit)", "(X Bootleg)", "(Original Mix)", "(club mix)", "(Dub Edit)", "(Radio Edit)", "(free download)".
- Keep "(Extended Mix)", "(VIP)", "(Instrumental)" in the title.
- Drop track numbers ("01 "), iTunes' " - Single" and " - EP" endings (with the dash), and an album that just repeats the title. "EP" that is part of a release's own name stays ("BURN EP", "Fake EP").
- Accented and special letters become plain ASCII where a plain equivalent exists (Ø→O, é→e, ū→u); keep scripts with no ASCII equivalent (大蛇丸) as they are.
- Keep the artist's own capitalization; don't change case.
- Never drop a word the source has unless a rule above removes it. If the file name has no title, or you can't tell which part is the artist, album or title, return the parser's name unchanged with confident=false.
- A style word inside a remix credit is not part of the remixer's name: "(Bukez Finezt Jersey Club Bootleg Remix)" is a remix by Bukez Finezt.
